# Task Plan: task0003 — Reader-level tests: visibility resume, undelivered snapshots, split OSC 9

## Goal

Reader-level tests drive the production visibility-resume path, the on-demand size rejection, deferred-snapshot eviction and the OSC 9 split, each with the PTY reader paused. They show that no captured chunk is re-applied after a resume snapshot (TS-2), that nothing is suppressed when no snapshot is delivered (TS-6), and that a split OSC 9 neither fires twice nor stitches with a later Detached period (TS-7).

## Design

Cross-task decisions D1 to D10 and the lock conventions are in IMPLEMENTATION.md. D5 (deferred commit at insertion), D6 (suppressed-chunk handling) and D9 (cases that record no boundary) are the decisions these tests pin.

### Common harness (test code only)
- Drive `pty_reader_loop` with the existing scripted reader, and pause it with the existing test-only pause hook at P2 on the chunk under test. Wait for the pause with a bounded `recv_timeout`; never order with sleeps.
- Async production paths (`handle_request_pane_snapshot`, the flush path) run in a tokio test while the reader waits on its std pause channel.
- Where the reference comparison applies, the client view is the snapshot chunk's decoded payload replayed with `reset_and_replay_segments`, followed by every later destination chunk in order. It is compared with a fresh `term_core` fed the raw stream once: visible rows, cursor position, scroll region and origin mode.
- A reader-spawn helper variant that starts the pane in a given `PaneOutputTarget` may be added next to the existing `spawn_reader_with_chunks`. Do not change that existing helper's behavior.

### Visibility resume (TS-2)
- The pane starts as `Detached { reason: HiddenByVisibility, owner: Some(S) }`.
- While the reader is paused at P2 on the chunk under test, reserve a slot on S and call the production `resume_pane_with_permit`, expecting `Resumed`. Then release the reader.
- `evaluate_output_target`'s `ResumeWithSnapshot` branch is not driven here. Its only production caller passes `visible = false`, so the branch is unreachable in production (IMPLEMENTATION.md D10).

### Undelivered snapshots (TS-6)
- **Size rejection**: the pane is `Connected(S)`, and its on-demand snapshot exceeds the single-frame limit (for example the extreme-dimension alt-screen fixture the existing oversize tests use). While the reader is paused at P2, `handle_request_pane_snapshot` rejects the request.
- **Eviction**: S's channel is full, so the paused pane's on-demand snapshot is deferred with a deferred boundary commit. Further on-demand requests for other panes then push it out of the `DeferredOutputQueue` under its existing backlog policy (as in the existing eviction test). Release the reader, then drain S.

### Split OSC 9 (TS-7)
- The OSC 9 introducer and part of its body are in the chunk under test; its terminator is in the next chunk.
- The chunk under test is suppressed under TS-3 conditions: while the reader is paused at P2 on a `Connected(S)` pane, the production `handle_request_pane_snapshot` delivers an on-demand snapshot to S. The next chunk is numbered above that snapshot's boundary, so it is not suppressed.
- After that, the pane is switched to `Detached` and more output containing a terminator is fed through the reader.

## Acceptance Criteria (MANDATORY)

- [ ] AC-1 (FR1, FR2, FR3; TS-2): with the reader paused at P2 on a chunk containing an insert-line sequence (`CSI L`), `resume_pane_with_permit` on a `Detached{HiddenByVisibility}` pane owned by S returns `Resumed`. After release:
  - S's channel holds the snapshot first;
  - the paused chunk's raw bytes never follow it;
  - the client view matches the raw-stream reference.
- [ ] AC-2 (sensitivity for AC-1): AC-1's test fails when the boundary record in `resume_pane_with_permit` is disabled. The implementer confirms this once by temporarily disabling the record and notes the result in the task's test record; the committed code keeps the record.
- [ ] AC-3 (FR5; TS-6, size rejection): with the reader paused at P2 on a `Connected(S)` pane whose on-demand snapshot exceeds the single-frame limit, `handle_request_pane_snapshot` enqueues no snapshot and records no boundary for S. After release, S's channel receives the paused chunk's exact bytes, then the following chunk, then the empty EOF chunk.
- [ ] AC-4 (FR5; TS-6, eviction): the paused pane's deferred on-demand snapshot is evicted from the `DeferredOutputQueue` before it ever enters S's channel. No boundary for S takes effect, and after release and draining, S's channel carries the paused chunk's exact bytes and the empty EOF chunk. The evicted snapshot is never delivered.
- [ ] AC-5 (FR6; TS-7, split across a suppressed and an unsuppressed chunk, under the Design section's TS-3 conditions):
  - the daemon's notification channel receives at most one notification for the split OSC 9;
  - the bytes delivered to S after the snapshot contain the OSC 9 exactly once as a complete sequence when concatenated, so a client fires at most once;
  - the total of daemon-side notifications plus complete OSC 9 sequences delivered to S for this OSC 9 is exactly one.
- [ ] AC-6 (FR6; TS-7, no stitching): after AC-5's sequence, the pane is switched to `Detached` and the reader is fed output that contains an OSC terminator but no OSC 9 introducer. The daemon's notification channel receives no notification. A separate complete OSC 9 fed during that same Detached period still produces exactly one notification whose text is that OSC 9's own body.
- [ ] AC-7 (TS-13, NFR5): all existing tests pass with their assertions and inputs unmodified, and `CARGO_TARGET_DIR=src-tauri/target cargo test --manifest-path src-tauri/Cargo.toml --lib` and `CARGO_TARGET_DIR=src-tauri/target cargo check --manifest-path src-tauri/Cargo.toml --no-default-features` both exit 0.

## Test Notes

- **AC-1**: the snapshot on S is a Snapshot-kind chunk carrying the encoded payload. Decode it with the protocol's snapshot-payload decoder before replaying it into the client `term_core`.
- **AC-3**: check "records no boundary" through the boundary record's existing covered-check operation for S and the paused chunk's number, as well as through delivery.
- **AC-4**: the reader's post-release insertion hits a full channel and waits for a slot through the existing reserved-slot path. Drain S from the test to let it proceed. Assert on the drained sequence after the reader has joined.
- **AC-5**: the suppressed chunk's FR10 replacement may legitimately re-deliver the OSC 9 prefix held as pending by the scrollback write filter, followed by the unsuppressed terminator chunk. Assert on the concatenated delivered bytes, not on chunk boundaries.
- **AC-6**: the stitching guard is the passthrough scanner's partial discard after a suppressed chunk (IMPLEMENTATION.md D6). Feed the Detached-period output through the reader so that the Detached arm's `capture_passthrough` runs.
- **Unrelated flakiness**: the `tabs.rs` replay tests and the `tmux_sockets` discover test are known to be flaky in parallel runs and are unrelated.
- **Formatting**: do not run a crate-wide formatter.
