# Task Plan: task0001 — A detection read detects every later compound and keeps the later carry

## Goal

In a detection read, `AltSpanTracker` detects every compound that follows the
first detected one in the same read, whatever its two screens say, and it
carries into the next read the item that the scan recorded after the last
detected compound. As a result, the two review findings 2139988314e6d6ac and
89b935844bd8bc3b no longer reproduce, and a mux pane ends in the plain tab's
OSC 7501 table and OSC 777 state for every SC-1z row and every delivery the
row lists.

## Design

### §1 Terms

- **Client rule**: term_core's view of the screen. A recognized switch
  pattern switches screens, except when its `ESC` sits in the designator
  slot right after `ESC (` or `ESC )`.
- **Shadow rule**: the shadow parser's view of the screen. Every `ESC`
  aborts what came before it, so a pattern in a designator slot also
  switches. Only ?1049 and ?47 switch.
- **Mismatched state**: the tracker's client-rule screen differs from its
  shadow-rule screen. **Matched state**: they agree.
- **Compound**: a DEC private set or reset that starts at a live `ESC`,
  holds 47, 1047 or 1049 among its parameters, and is none of the six switch
  patterns (REQUIREMENTS.md, glossary).
- **Detection read**: a read in which the scan detected at least one
  compound.
- **Carried item**: what a read hands to the next one. It is one of: a
  partial switch (live or designator kind, at most 7 bytes), the designator
  wait, or the DEC private CSI parse state.

### §2 Detection within a read (FR1)

1. At a compound's terminator (`h` or `l`), the compound is detected when
   either of these holds:
   - an earlier compound in the same read was already detected, or
   - the tracker is in a mismatched state.
2. Every detected compound sets the client-rule screen to its terminator's
   direction (`h` the alternate screen, `l` the main screen) and moves the
   live run the way the first detection does today.
3. While nothing has been detected in the read, a compound that ends in a
   matched state is not detected (predecessor A2). This also holds for a
   compound whose CSI parse was carried in from the previous read.
4. The "already detected" status belongs to one read. Every read starts
   with it cleared.
5. Switch patterns and RIS that follow a detected compound in the same read
   keep their current handling.

### §3 End of a detection read (FR2, FR3)

1. **Unchanged (FR3)**:
   - The read's spans are the A3 fallback judged on the client-rule screen
     at the read's end: the whole read on the main screen, none otherwise.
   - The shadow-rule screen takes the parser's state after the read.
   - One adoption is counted for the read, however many compounds it
     detected.
   - The post-read cross-check does not apply to the read.
2. **Changed (FR2)**: the carried item that the scan recorded at the read's
   end is kept for the next read. It is no longer cleared.
3. **Why this keeps only the later state (SPEC A1)**: the scan consumes the
   incoming carried item at its start and records a new one only where it
   stops at the read's end. The kept item therefore always comes from bytes
   after the last detected compound. State from before the compound is
   already gone.
4. The next read resolves the kept item exactly as it resolves an item
   carried from a read without detection:
   - a partial switch is completed, extended or diverges
   - after the designator wait, the next byte is the designator
   - a CSI parse continues, and its terminator is judged by §2 with the new
     read's own detection status
5. Examples (SPEC FR2), each after M1:
   - `C7h ESC (` | `ESC c A BEL`: the `ESC c` sits in the designator slot.
     It is no RIS for the client, and the mark is not live.
   - `C1l ESC [?10` | `47h A BEL`: the partial completes ?1047h. The client
     is on the alternate screen, and the mark is not live.
   - `C7h ESC` | `c A BEL`: the carried lone `ESC` and the `c` are a RIS
     (predecessor FR2), and the mark is live.

## Test Notes

- **Where the tests go**: the tracker tests go into
  `tests/altscreen_split_read.rs`, in a new section after the predecessor's
  one. They reuse its rig, its delivery set, its well-formedness check and
  its client-rule walk. The walk that takes several override points is a new
  helper. The existing walk keeps its signature and its results, and may
  delegate to the new one. AC-5 goes into `daemon/tests.rs`, next to the
  K38-K57 list. The K58-K64 list there needs a delivery builder that accepts
  two cut ranges, for K60 and K61.
- **Per-delivery oracle for AC-3**: walk the reads in order and keep the
  tracker's two screens. The shadow-rule screen follows the shadow rule of
  Design §1 (switch patterns of ?1049 and ?47, also in a designator slot,
  and RIS, also in a designator slot) and takes the parser's state after
  every detection read and every cross-check read. Each read falls under
  exactly one of three rules:
  1. **Detection read.** The read holds the terminator of a compound that
     §2 detects. Its spans are the whole read when the client-rule screen at
     the read's end is the main screen, and none otherwise. It counts one
     adoption.
  2. **Cross-check read.** The read detects nothing, and the parser's state
     after it differs from the shadow-rule screen the scan ends in. Its
     spans are the whole read only when the parser stood on the main screen
     before and after the read, and none otherwise. It counts one adoption,
     and both screens take the parser's state.
  3. **Any other read.** Its spans are the client-rule walk of the whole
     stream, restricted to the read and without the read's undecided tail.
     The walk overrides the client screen right after the terminator of
     every detected compound (to its direction) and at the end of every
     cross-check read (to the parser's state). A compound that is neither
     detected nor followed by a cross-check changes nothing.

  Literal expectations are equally acceptable for short cases.
- **Accepted limitation (IMPLEMENTATION.md Open Questions)**: for M1 +
  l-form + C7h and M2 + h-form + C7l, a delivery that splits the two
  compounds leaves the second one undetected. The oracle above gives the
  tracker's behavior there, not term_core's, and the test pins that
  behavior (predecessor A2).
- **Excluded cuts**: K60 and K61 list no cuts from 20 to 29
  (IMPLEMENTATION.md D5). If any listed delivery of any row fails, report
  the row and the delivery as a plan deviation. Do not drop the delivery.
- **TDD order**: write these first. Each must fail on the unmodified
  tracker:
  - the AC-5 rows K58 in one read (it ends empty instead of root `working`)
    and K62 cut at 20 (it ends empty)
  - the AC-1 reproduction 1 case and the AC-2 reproduction 2 case
  - the renamed test's third case (the partial length is 0 instead of 5)
- **Edge cases worth naming** (SPEC edge cases): three or more alternating
  compounds in one read; a compound followed by a full switch pattern in the
  same read; a detection read that ends in each carried-item kind; a lone
  `ESC` then `c` (a RIS); adoption counts that differ by delivery (one when
  the two compounds share a read, two when a cut separates them).
- **Plan deviation**: report a failing existing test as a plan deviation.
  Do not edit it.
